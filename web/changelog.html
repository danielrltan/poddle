What's new

What changed in Poddle, newest first. Changes to how Poddle handles information are noted here on the day they take effect, with a link to the updated Privacy Policy or Terms of Use.

September 29, 2026

Friends

Signed in with a username? Open Friends on the home screen, or from the settings card during a game, to find players by username and send them a friend request. Your friends list shows who is online and what they are doing, such as Playing Matt or In a Ranked match, but never which court they are on. Select a name in search, in your friends list or on your court to open the same profile card as on the leaderboard, now with a friend button and, for a friend, whether they are online. A player who is not on the leaderboard shows their rank emblem without the six stats; for a player who has turned Show me on the global leaderboard off, only their friends see those. You can have up to 100 friends. Declining a request or removing a friend is not announced to the other player.

Ranked is for signed-in players

To play Ranked, sign in and pick a username. Every Ranked trophy now belongs to an account, which is what the global leaderboard ranks. Guests can still play Quick play, courts, tournaments and Matt. If you earned trophies as a guest, they move to your account when you sign in on the same browser.

New personal bests show during a match

When a rally or a swing beats your best, a small note says so at the end of that point.

Wins over new players count

A win over a player who has just started now counts toward your stats and trophies in full.

Tap a name on the leaderboard

Every name on the global leaderboard now opens that player's profile card: their rank, trophies, hardest Matt beaten and the same stats as a shared card. Turn off Show me on the global leaderboard to hide yours.

More stats on your player card

Shared cards and leaderboard profile cards now lead with your win rate against people (with your record under it), your time on court and your best win streak against people, followed by return rate, points won, longest rally, fastest swing, winners, aces, smashes and tournament titles. Every card shows the same eleven, so cards are easy to compare. The win streak on a card now counts only matches against people, like the leaderboard.

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use were updated to match: anyone can open the profile card of a player on the leaderboard, your friends and friend requests are saved with your account, and your friends see when you are online.

September 28, 2026

Plainer wording across the game and site.

A global leaderboard

A new Leaderboard page on the home screen ranks every signed-in player in the world on three boards: Ranked trophies, longest rally and best win streak against people. Your stats shows your trophy place beside your rank, for example Champion II #301. Only signed-in players with a username are listed, and you can turn Show me on the global leaderboard off on the Leaderboard page.

A new rank, Master, and medals that grow

Ranked now has eight ranks: Master sits between Diamond and Champion. Every rank below Pro has three divisions, and its medal grows with each one: a second rim at II, gems and a glow at III. Your trophies are unchanged; if you had 750 or more, your rank was worked out again from them.

Pick Matt's level in the Ranked warm-up

While you wait for a Ranked opponent, you can now set Matt to any level with the Difficulty setting or the 1 to 4 keys. A win pays trophies only against your rank's level of Matt or a harder one; an easier Matt is practice.

Pro has no divisions

Pro is now a single rank with no I, II or III. Pro players are told apart by their place on the global leaderboard, for example Pro #12.

Your stats are always saved; delete them any time on the privacy page

The Save my stats switch is gone: the matches you play are always saved to your stats, with a random identifier in this browser or, if you are signed in, to your account. You can still download a copy or delete your data at any time in the Your data box of the Privacy Policy, and Ranked no longer asks you to turn anything on. If you had turned stats off, the game forgets that setting the next time you open it.

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use were updated to match.

September 27, 2026

Ranked

A new Ranked mode. Press Find a match to play another player in a best of 3 (games to 7). You can warm up with Matt while you wait. Win trophies to climb seven ranks from Bronze to Pro, three divisions each. Ranks up to Platinum are never lost once reached. Your rank emblem is shown beside your name to your opponent and to anyone watching a Ranked court; everything else about your record stays private. The home screen now has Quick play and Ranked up top, with Courts, Play a bot and Your stats under them.

Your stats now shows your Ranked rank. The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use were updated: your rank emblem is shown to your opponent and anyone watching a Ranked match.

Return rate and more stats

Your stats now shows your return rate (how many of your opponent's balls you hit back), plus winners, aces, smashes, total hits, points won and time on court, tracked from September 27, 2026 in every match that counts toward your stats.

Share your card

Press Share card in Your stats to copy a poddleball.com link that shows up as a picture of your player card wherever you paste it: your Ranked rank and its emblem, your trophies, your Matt badge and the same six stats on every card, so cards are easy to compare. If you are signed in and have picked a username, it carries your username; otherwise it says Poddle player. Anyone with the link can see the card, and Stop sharing turns the link off.

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use were updated for the new stats and the share card too.

September 25, 2026

Your stats

A new Your stats page keeps your record: wins and losses against Matt at every level (Rookie, Club, Tour, Pro) with the date you first beat him and your streaks, your record against people, and your personal bests for the longest rally and the fastest swing. Stats are saved with a random identifier in this browser; you can turn this off on the Privacy Policy page, and download or delete your data from the Your stats page. Only you can see them.

Sign in with Google

Optional. Signing in keeps your stats on any device and gives you a username. Poddle keeps only an identifier from Google, never your name, email or picture.

The Privacy Policy and Terms of Use were updated for both.

September 24, 2026

New result card

The end-of-match card now shows a medal, a crown for the winner and match stats (longest rally, smashes, best run), and plays a sound for a win or a loss.

Camera explainer

Before Poddle asks to use the camera, a one-time screen says what it is for (finding where you stand) and that the video never leaves your computer. If you would rather not use the camera, choose Auto and the game moves you to the ball.

Terms of Use and Privacy Policy

Both pages are published and linked from every page.

September 23, 2026

Tournaments

Create one and share its code. Players can warm up with Matt while they wait, then the host starts a knockout bracket. With an odd number of players, Matt fills the empty spot.

Courts

Courts lists every public court, Open or Full, with search. Join or Watch from the list, or enter a code. Spectators can ask to take Matt's seat.

Better lobs

An underhand swing now plays as a lob every time.

September 22, 2026

Smash, slice and block

A smash takes a fast arm stroke, a slice takes a deliberate cut, and at the kitchen you can hold the paddle up to block. Your phone flashes in the color of the ball's trail when you hit.

Settings, regrouped

Settings are now grouped into You, Paddle, Match, Screen and Sound. Switch between phone and AirPod at any time, and send sound to your speakers instead of the AirPod.

September 21, 2026

Your phone is the paddle

Scan the code on the set-up screen and swing your phone. Any computer, any phone, nothing to install. Poddle moved to poddleball.com.

Poddle Helper

On a Mac, one AirPod can be the paddle instead, through a small open-source menu-bar app you download from this site.

September 20, 2026

Poddle

Built for Hack the North 2026: Wii Sports-style pickleball where the controller is an AirPod.
