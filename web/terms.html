3. Play safely

Poddle is a physical game. You swing a real phone or AirPod like a paddle, and with a webcam you step side to side. Things can go wrong: a phone can slip out of a hand, a swing can hit a lamp, and a step can catch a rug.

Before and while you play

  • Clear space all around you, including behind you and above you. Look before every swing.
  • Hold on tight. Phones and AirPods don't come with a strap, so use a firm grip, and a wrist strap or grippy case if you have one. Keep dry hands.
  • You swing toward your screen. Stand well back from the laptop or TV (the webcam needs you a couple of metres away anyway), and never play with someone between you and the screen or sitting within reach.
  • Never throw or let go of your phone or AirPod on purpose. The game never needs you to.
  • An AirPod is small: if it flies off, find it before a young child or pet does.
  • Don't play with a phone whose glass is cracked.
  • Watch for people, pets and breakable things: TVs, screens, windows, lamps, glasses and furniture. Keep other people, especially children, out of swinging range.
  • Play on a flat floor that isn't slippery, in suitable shoes or bare feet, with nothing to trip over.
  • Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or discomfort, and take breaks. If you have a health condition or injury that physical activity could affect, check with a doctor first.
  • Don't play somewhere you must pay attention to something else, like while driving or near stairs or roads.

Permission prompts

Poddle may ask for your camera, your phone's motion sensors, keeping the screen awake, local network access (for AirPods) and, only if you pick a speaker in Settings, the microphone. Allow them only on poddleball.com. Poddle never asks for your password, contacts, photos, location or payment details. The Privacy Policy explains each one.

5. Public courts and spectators

  • Listed courts appear in the public court list, with both players' names, the score and how many people are watching. Anyone can watch.
  • Courts made with a code are unlisted, not locked. Anyone with the 4-character code or link can join an empty seat or watch. Only share it with people you want to play with.
  • Spectators see your name, score and every move and swing. Spectators' names are shown to the players when they start watching or ask to play, and on any emote they send.
  • Tournaments: the host's name is listed while people join, and anyone with the tournament code sees every player's name and the bracket.
  • Your phone pairing QR code and code link a phone to your game. Don't show or share them, for example on a stream, and don't scan someone else's without asking.
  • Poddle does not record or replay matches. But anyone watching could film their own screen, so play knowing others can see.
