Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026. Effective: September 24, 2026.

Poddle is a free pickleball game that runs in your browser at poddleball.com. This Privacy Policy explains, in plain language, what information the game uses, why it is used, who can see it and how long it is kept. It also covers the Poddle Helper application for Mac. The rules for using Poddle are set out in our Terms of Use.

Summary

  • No accounts, cookies, advertising or analytics. We never sell your information or share it for advertising purposes.
  • Your webcam video remains on your computer. It is processed within your browser to determine approximately where you are standing. No video, images or face data are transmitted or saved. We do not identify anyone.
  • Your position and your paddle's motion are transmitted live to our game server and relayed to your opponent and anyone watching, so that the match can be played. They are not stored.
  • Your display name is publicly visible to other players and spectators. Please do not use your full real name.
  • Our server holds your IP address and display name in memory only, in order to operate the game and prevent abuse. We maintain no database, and all of this information is erased when the server restarts.
  • Questions or requests: hello@danielrltan.com.

For teens and parents

  1. Use a nickname rather than your real name. It is visible to strangers.
  2. Camera video never leaves your computer. Only an approximate position is transmitted.
  3. There is no chat, profile or account. The game server discards your name when the match ends or the server restarts.
  4. Anyone with a court code can watch or take an empty seat, so please share codes only with friends. Do not display your phone's pairing code on a stream.
  5. Under 18? Please ask a parent before playing or installing Poddle Helper.
  6. For questions or deletion requests, please contact hello@danielrltan.com.

2. Information we use and why

Poddle does not require registration. We do not ask for your email address, age, location, phone number or payment details. The table below sets out all of the information the game uses.

We use your IP address only to keep a free game online without spam, flooding or attacks. We do not use it to determine your location or your identity.

A display name is required to take a seat or watch, and your IP address is necessary for any website to reach you. Without them, you cannot play online. All other information, such as camera and motion data, is optional or used only while you play.

In the background, the game's connection to our server includes your display name, tab ID, court code and score, so that you can be returned to your court after a reconnection. The link in your browser's address bar contains only the court code.

Device permissions the game may request

  • Camera, on your computer, only when you take a seat to play. See Your webcam.
  • Motion and orientation, on your phone, when you tap Start on the phone page. On an iPhone, this is the Motion & Orientation prompt. Motion is read only on the secure (https) page.
  • Keep screen awake, on your phone, so that it does not lock during a match. The phone may also vibrate to indicate a hit.
  • Microphone, only if you open Settings and choose to select a sound output (speakers). Browsers display speaker names only after this permission has been granted. The game stops the microphone immediately. No audio is listened to, recorded or transmitted.
  • Local network (in Chrome), if you play with an AirPod. This permission allows the page to communicate with Poddle Helper on your own Mac. If you have played before and have not chosen between phone and AirPod, or if you chose AirPod, the page may check in the background whether Poddle Helper is running on your Mac, and Chrome may ask you about local network access.

You may decline any of these permissions, or disable them later in your browser or phone settings. Without the camera, the game moves your player automatically (Auto). Without phone motion or an AirPod, you may still watch matches.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising purposes, build profiles or make automated decisions about you. We do not use your information to train artificial intelligence. We do not run advertisements, analytics tools, tracking pixels or social media plug-ins on Poddle.

3. Your webcam

If you play with Body movement, the game uses your computer's webcam to determine where you are standing. The process is described in detail below.

  • Before anything is requested, Poddle shows a screen explaining why the camera is used, and you choose Allow camera or Play without camera. Only if you choose Allow does your browser ask for permission. Camera access is requested only when you take a seat to play; spectators are never asked. The camera captures video only, without sound.
  • The video is analysed within your browser, on your computer, by Google's open-source MediaPipe software, which Poddle hosts itself. For each video frame, the software locates a face and a few points on it (eyes, nose, mouth and ears) or, if it cannot detect your face, points on your body such as your nose and shoulders.
  • From those points, the game retains only a single smoothed centre point, representing approximately where your head and shoulders are in the image. The points themselves are discarded immediately, frame by frame, and never leave your browser.
  • No video, photo, frame or list of face or body points is ever transmitted to our server or to anyone else, and none is saved.
  • We do not recognise or identify anyone. We do not create, collect or store any face template, faceprint or record of face geometry.
  • MediaPipe, as published by Google, can send usage statistics to Google. We have disabled this feature in our copy, so it sends nothing to Google or to anyone else.

What is transmitted: the centre point is converted into your player's position on the court. That position, together with your paddle's angle, is sent to our game server approximately 60 times per second. The server relays it to your opponent and to anyone watching that court, so that they can see you move. It is not stored.

Your choice: the webcam is not required to play. Choose Play without camera (or, when your browser asks, select Do not Allow or Block), and the game will move your player automatically (Auto). Play without camera is remembered on that browser, and the camera is not requested again unless you turn it on. Selecting Auto in Settings does not turn the camera off: once you have granted access, the camera remains on until you close the Poddle tab, although its video still never leaves your computer. You may disable camera access at any time in your browser's site settings.

4. Information visible to other players

Poddle is played with, and in view of, other people. Please choose a display name that you are comfortable with strangers seeing. Do not use your full real name, particularly if you are under 18.

  • Listed courts: anyone who opens the court list can see the names of both players, the score and the number of people watching.
  • Unlisted courts (created with a code): these do not appear in the list, but anyone who has the 4-character code or link can take an empty seat or watch. Because codes are short, a code should be treated like a room number, not a password.
  • Tournaments: the host's name is listed while players join. Anyone with the tournament code can see all players' names and the bracket.
  • On a court: players and spectators can see the players' names, scores, movements and swings. Players can also see the name of each spectator who begins watching or requests to play, and everyone on the court can see the name of any spectator who sends an emote.

Poddle has no chat feature. Emotes are limited to a fixed set of 7 emoji.

7. Data retention

  • Display names, IP addresses, court and tournament details: held in the game server's memory for as long as your court, tournament or anti-spam cooldown lasts, and at the latest until the server restarts. The server writes none of this information to disk. The server restarts when we update the game, or when it goes to sleep because no one is connected. If you are in the middle of a match during a restart, your own tab sends back your court code, display name and score so that the match can continue.
  • Motion, position and swing data: relayed live and not stored.
  • Webcam video: never leaves your computer and is never saved.
  • Activity logs: the server writes brief activity entries, such as "court joined" or "tournament started: 4 players", with court codes. Our logs do not include display names, IP addresses or tab IDs. Fly.io's own network and platform logs may record IP addresses and web addresses. We do not export, copy or archive any of these logs. Fly.io retains them for approximately 7 days, after which they are deleted automatically.
  • Browser storage: remains on your device until you clear it or, in the case of the tab ID and pairing code, until you close the tab.
  • Emails you send us: kept until your request has been resolved, and then deleted within 12 months.

Poddle is intended for people 13 years of age and older. If you are under 18, you must have your parent's or guardian's permission to play. If you live in Quebec and are under 14, a parent or guardian must consent to the information handling described in this policy before you play. 11. Security

We protect information by keeping as little of it as possible. The website and the game connection use encrypted connections (HTTPS). Game data is held in memory rather than in a database. Webcam video never leaves your device. Phone motion data is validated and relayed only to the single tab that paired with that phone.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach of security involving personal information creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify the privacy regulators as required by law (within 72 hours where the GDPR applies), including the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. Because we hold no contact details for players, we will also post a notice on the Poddle home page. We will keep a record of every breach.

12. Poddle Helper for Mac

Poddle Helper is an optional, free and open-source Mac application that allows an AirPod to serve as your paddle.

  • It reads only the motion of your headphones, using Apple's motion framework: the direction in which the AirPod points, how quickly it rotates and moves, and which earbud it is. macOS requests your permission (Motion & Fitness) first.
  • It passes that motion data only to other programs on the same Mac (it is never transmitted over the internet), and only while a page is connected to it. It accepts connections from web pages at Poddle's own addresses (poddleball.com and its earlier poddle.fly.dev address), pages served from your own Mac, and local applications on your Mac.
  • It makes no internet connections of its own and collects no information. It contains no analytics. Its menu links simply open web pages in your browser.
  • Once the motion data reaches Poddle in your browser, it is used in the same way as phone motion, as described above.

Its source code is available on GitHub under the MIT license.
