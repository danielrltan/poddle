        Getting ready

        Let the game see where you stand

        Poddle uses this computer’s webcam, not your phone, to see where you are. Step left or right and your player moves with you.

        • Why it’s neededStepping moves you on court. Without the camera, the game runs you to the ball and you only swing.
        • Stays on this computerThe video is read in this browser and never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Only where you stand goes to the game, as your player’s position.
        • Your browser will askPress Allow when it does. To play without it later, pick Auto in Settings, under Move.

        In Chrome

        1. Click the icon at the left of the address bar.
        2. Turn Camera on, or open Site settings and set Camera to Allow.
        3. Come back here and press Try again.

        In Edge

        1. Click the icon at the left of the address bar.
        2. Open Permissions for this site and set Camera to Allow.
        3. Come back here and press Try again.

        In Safari

        1. In the menu bar, choose Safari > Settings for poddleball.com.
        2. Set Camera to Allow.
        3. Come back here and press Try again.

        In Firefox

        1. Click the crossed-out camera icon in the address bar.
        2. Next to Use the camera, click the × beside Blocked.
        3. Come back here and press Try again.

        On a Mac

        1. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.
        2. Turn on your browser.
        3. If it says so, quit the browser and open it again.

        On Windows

        1. Open Settings > Privacy & security > Camera.
        2. Turn on Camera access and Let desktop apps access your camera.
        3. Come back here and press Try again.

        On an iPad

        1. Open the Settings app, then Apps > your browser > Camera.
        2. Choose Allow.
        3. Come back here and reload the page.
